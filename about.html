About us

Little Planet Academy · Giginyu, Kano

Who we are

Little Planet Academy is more than a school — it is a second home where children are loved, guided and prepared for a bright future.

We provide affordable, high-quality education that develops academic ability, moral character and social confidence.

Our mission

Every child is a unique star. Our mission is to nurture the whole child — mind, heart and character — in a safe and caring environment.

Located in Giginyu, Kano, we combine dedicated teachers, interactive lessons and strong Islamic values so every pupil is ready for the next stage of life.

What makes us different

Blended curriculum — Nigerian national curriculum combined with international early-years frameworks.

Interactive teaching — activity-driven lessons, not dry lectures.

Character first — moral values, community awareness and life-readiness skills alongside academics.
